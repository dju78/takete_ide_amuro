Summary 
The Takete-Ide website structure is largely in place. The main requirement now is to collect, 
verify and upload authentic community information. 
The immediate focus should be on: 
• Centenary 2026 information 
• Development project records 
• Missing photographs 
• Traditional Council information 
• TIPU branch information 
• School information 
• Community news and announcements 
Historical information such as family histories, compounds, Oríkì, past Olu’des, oral 
histories and archival documents can then be developed progressively as part of the 
Takete-Ide Digital Heritage Archive.
