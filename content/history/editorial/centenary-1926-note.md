# Editorial Note: The 1926 Settlement Date and Centenary 2026

**Status**: SUBJECT TO COMMUNITY/CENTENARY LEADERSHIP CONFIRMATION

---

## 1. Core Historical Distinction

A critical finding from the community historical manuscript (`takete-history-original.md`) is that **Takete-Ide was NOT founded in 1926**.

The historical manuscript explicitly describes a much older community trajectory spanning multiple centuries and several sequential settlements:
1. **Amuro-Odo**: Ancient ancestral home shared by Amuro kinsmen before the 19th-century Nupe incursions.
2. **Igbo Ide (Igbo Idera)**: First forested place of refuge east of Amuro-Odo, shared initially with the people of Otafun.
3. **Ilu-Oke**: The fortified upland/hilltop town where the community defended itself, established protective sanctuaries (Agbagba Ide), and lived for generations through 19th-century regional warfare.
4. **Okeata / Surrounding Uplands**: Mountain redoubts occupied during heightened conflict.
5. **The Present Low-Plain Settlement (Ileteju / Takete-Idera)**: Descended from the hills to the plains *“in 1926 or thereabouts”*.

---

## 2. What the 1926 Date Represents

The manuscript states:
> *“The migrations from the uplands to the low plains took place in 1926 or thereabouts, according to records. The first child to be born in the new location was Pa. George Fiki.”*

Therefore:
- **1926 marks the historic descent from the upland settlement of Ilu-Oke to the fertile low plain** where modern Takete-Ide stands today.
- The peace, safety from cavalry raids, and fertile farming grounds allowed the community to move down permanently from the hills, initially calling the plains *Ileteju* before reaffirming the beloved name *Takete-Idera* (or *Takete-Ide*).
- **Takete-Ide is an ancient community**, not a 100-year-old settlement.

---

## 3. Recommended Centenary 2026 Framing

To ensure historical accuracy and prevent public misunderstanding, website copy and promotional material for the 2026 Centenary must avoid any claim that the town itself is only 100 years old.

### Recommended Public Formulations:
- **“Takete-Ide Centenary 2026 — Commemorating a Century at the Present Settlement”**
- **“From the Hills to the Present Settlement: c.1926–2026”**
- **“Celebrating 100 Years of Settlement on the Plains: 1926–2026”**

### Forbidden Public Formulations:
- ❌ *“Takete-Ide was founded in 1926.”*
- ❌ *“Celebrating 100 years since the founding of Takete-Ide.”*
- ❌ *“Takete-Ide is 100 years old.”*
